Introducing Men of Valor: Our Full Patriarchs Series

Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob are just the beginning — here's what's coming next in the Men of Valor collection.

By the Twelve Scents Team·May 5, 2026·2 min read
Coming Soon

When we launched Book One with Abraham, we knew we weren't publishing a single story — we were opening the front door to a whole family line. Today we're officially naming what's been in the works since the very beginning: Men of Valor, the complete patriarchs series.

Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob are the first three covers, already available for pre-sale. But the family tree doesn't stop there, and neither will we.

What's coming next

Men of Valor will continue to expand with additional patriarchs and the men whose obedience shaped Israel's earliest generations — each one illustrated with the same historical care as Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and each one paired, where their stories intersect, with the Daughters of Scripture companion collection.

Our goal is a full shelf: a complete, Scripture-rooted family library where a child can trace an unbroken line of brown-skinned heroes from Genesis forward, without ever losing themselves in the gaps between books.

"The seed of the righteous shall be delivered." Proverbs 11:21 · KJV 1611

Why we're announcing it now

We're sharing this early, before every cover is finished, because we want families to plan their shelf around it — pre-ordering the books that exist now, and knowing exactly which brothers, sons, and grandsons are still on their way.

Follow the Journal or join our reading ministry newsletter below, and you'll be the first to see each new cover the moment it's revealed.
